ADILUHUNG ARMCHAIRS AND CHAIRS

Derived from the Javanese word meaning “noble” or “sublime,” Adiluhung is more than a piece of furniture; it is a reverence to heritage. Each curve and joint reflects the wisdom of generations of artisans, hand-carved and assembled using age-old woodworking traditions passed down through centuries

FUNCTION
Adiluhung available in Chairs and armchairs style. It can easily be a dining chairs, desk chair or even lounge chair options. We can customize the measurements to suit your needs.

MEASUREMENTS
60 (width) x 55 (length) x 81 (height) cm

UPHOLSTERY OPTIONS
we have a wide fabric variety option, from water and stain repellant fabric option to genuine I talian leather options

Material and finishes
Adiluhung armchair and chairs available in natural to darker finishes but we suggested to have the closed pore finish to show the smoothness of the body and the beautiful grain of Precious Solid Indonesian Teakwood (only available in Solid Teakwood option)
