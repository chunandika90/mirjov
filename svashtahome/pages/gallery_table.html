ABIYASA DINING TABLE

Handcrafted from Indonesian precious solid teakwood, Abiyasa is finished in our signature Natural finish, created to celebrate the untouched beauty of the material. The soft, golden hue reveals the wood’s expressive grain, knots, and rich organic patterns — no two pieces are ever alike

FUNCTION
Abiyasa dining table is made to showcasing the finest of teakwood. It can be made into a dining table or meeting table with customization to the measurement.

MEASUREMENTS
Rectangular (with 2 base) 300 (length) x 120 (width) x 75 (height) cm and smaller version of 250 (length x 100 (width) x 75 (height) cm for dining table and for round table with the measurement of diameter 150 to 180 cm x 75 cm height

TOP TABLE MATERIALS
You have options of GLASS TABLE TOP to STONE TOP or WOODEN TOP with veneer options.

MATERIAL AND FINISHES
Abiyasa table available in natural to darker finishes but we suggested to have the fine opened pore finish to show the smoothness of the body and the beautiful grain of Precious Solid Indonesian Teakwood (available in Teakwood veneer and Sungkai veneer option.

ANDA DINING TABLE

With soft geometry and quiet confidence, Anda welcomes the sitter with open arms and gentle contours. The backrest curves inward just enough to embrace. The proportions feel tailored, human, close — like a conversation that doesn’t need to raise its voice. Anda is both understated and undeniably present — a piece that makes you feel seen without asking to be seen

FUNCTION
Anda table is so versatile, you can use one base and make it into round table (up to whatever size you need) and you can use two base and make it into an oval or rectangular table with rounded corners. You can make a shorter one and make it into a coffee table

MEASUREMENTS
BASE : DIAM. 160 TOP, DIAM 60 BASE cm Table top options : Round with Diameter 160 cm ; Rectangular (with 2 base) 300x120x75 cm.

TOP TABLE MATERIALS
We have a wide fabric variety option, from water and stain repellant fabric option to genuine I talian leather options.

MATERIAL AND FINISHES
Anda table available in natural to darker finishes but we suggested to have the fine opened pore finish to show the smoothness of the body and the beautiful grain of Precious Solid I ndonesian Teakwood (available in Teakwood veneer and White Oak veneer option).

AGUNA TABLE

The Aguna Table is not merely furniture—it is a dialogue between material and maker. Our artisans work in quiet harmony with the wood, preserving its essence while shaping it into a statement of elegance and quiet luxury. The result is a piece that breathes, ages gracefully, and tells a story only true teak can. Clean lines and purposeful simplicity define the silhouette—allowing the teak’s innate beauty to resonate. Whether as a dining centerpiece or a contemplative console, the Aguna Table anchors the room with presence, yet never overwhelms

FUNCTION
Aguna table is a round table. It can be made into a dining table or coffee table and side table with customization to the measurement.

Measurements
Round coffee table or side table with the measurement of diameter 40 to 110 cm x 50 cm height; for round dining table with the measurement of diameter 150 to 180 cm x 75 cm height

TOP TABLE MATERIALS
You have options of GLASS TABLE TOP to STONE TOP or WOODEN TOP with veneer options.

MATERIAL AND FINISHES
Aguna table available in natural to darker finishes but we suggested to have the exotic opened pore finish to show the smoothness of the body and the beautiful grain of Precious Solid Indonesian Teakwood (available in Teakwood veneer and Sungkai veneer option).
