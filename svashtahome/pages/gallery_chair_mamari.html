MAMARI ARMCHAIRS AND CHAIRS

Mamari is designed as a gentle embrace — its curved back and flowing armrests extend with tender openness, offering a seat that welcomes rather than commands. The silhouette is rounded, fluid, and grounded, evoking a sense of refuge and repose. It is a chair not only for sitting, but for settling into — a moment of pause amid movement, a space of quiet connection.
