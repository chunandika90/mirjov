KOTHAK NIGHT TABLE

Crafted from sustainably sourced Indonesian teakwood veneer, the Kothak’s architectural silhouette reveals the natural richness and complexity of the grain. Its surface features precision marquetry—a subtle geometric composition that reflects hours of careful workmanship and our signature attention to detail. Beneath its calm exterior lies a soft-close drawer, engineered for modern ease and silent efficiency. Whether used to cradle your nightly reads or to hold life’s smaller essentials, every motion is smooth, thoughtful, and satisfying.

FUNCTION
Kothak cabinet is made to showcasing the finest of teakwood. It can be made into night table or cabinet with customization to the measurement.

MEASUREMENTS
Rectangular size is 60 (length) x 40 (width) x 50 (height) cm

TOP TABLE MATERIALS
You have options of GLASS TABLE TOP to STONE TOP or WOODEN TOP with veneer options.

MATERIAL AND FINISHES
Kothak night table available in natural to darker finishes but we suggested to have the fine opened pore finish to show the smoothness of the body and the beautiful grain of Precious Solid Indonesian Teakwood (available in Teakwood veneer and Sungkai veneer option).
