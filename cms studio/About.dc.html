ABOUT THE STUDIO

PRINCIPAL & CHIEF ARCHITECT

Ryan Dharmansyah

B.Arch, M.Arch — University of New South Wales

Born in Indonesia and raised in Sydney, Ryan trained as an architect across leading Australian practices before founding Studio Dinding, specializing in innovative, energy-conscious architecture.

He believes the spaces we occupy shape how we behave — so every project is organized and optimized for genuine wellbeing, not just appearance.

CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Melita Lumanto

B.Des (Interior), M.Des (Lighting) — Swinburne / UTS Sydney

Trained as an interior architect before specializing in lighting design, Melita brings a rare understanding of how light shapes form, materiality and mood in a space.

Her work spans residential, hospitality and retail — including collaborations on projects in Singapore and Sydney — and she believes every good design begins with genuine listening.

CONSTRUCTION MANAGER

Henry Chandra

Bachelor of Civil Engineering — University of New South Wales

Henry began his career as a junior quantity surveyor in Jakarta before rising to lead site teams across the studio's projects.

As Construction Manager, he oversees planning, budgeting and on-site execution — making sure every design is delivered exactly as drawn, on time.
